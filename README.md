# Tally — Your Private Training & Body Log

**Free for everyone, for good.** No account, no ads, no subscription, no premium tier.

Tally is a self-calculating ledger. You write down what you eat, what you weigh and what you lift,
and it does the maths for you: a maintenance calorie estimate calibrated from your own food logs and
weight trend, a daily protein target, next-session suggestions for every lift, and weekly sets per
muscle. The goal is simple: nobody should need a stack of expensive subscriptions just to record
their training and see what it means.

Bodyweight, food, lifts, girths, mobility and cardio, all in one place, all on your own device.

## Use it

Open **https://popo54321.github.io/tally/** on your phone, then add it to your home screen. It runs
full screen with no browser bars, works with no signal, and behaves like any other app.

- **Android** — tap **Install** on the banner, or in Settings. If neither shows, use Chrome's **⋮**
  menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone / iPad** — tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Safari always works; on iOS 16.4
  and later Chrome and Edge can do it too. The home-screen app keeps its own data, separate from the
  browser, so if you have already logged in the browser, save a backup file and import it there.
- **Laptop** — it just works in the browser. Install it from the address bar if you want.

There is also a native Android app, Tally Pro: download the `.apk` from the
[latest release](https://github.com/popo54321/tally/releases/latest) and install it over any
earlier version. Your data stays.

## Your data

**Everything stays on your device.** There is no account, no server, and the app makes no network
request of its own — once it has loaded, it never talks to anything. Nobody, including whoever
hosts this page, can see what you log.

That cuts both ways: **nothing is backed up for you.**

- **Settings → Sync Between Devices → Save backup file** writes a `.json` you can keep anywhere.
- Do that every few weeks, and before changing phone, clearing your browser, or deleting the app.
  The app reminds you when one is due.
- To move to another device: **Save backup file** on the old one, **Import from file** on the new
  one, then **Load imported data** twice to confirm.

On iPhone in particular, Safari can clear a site's storage if you go a long stretch without opening
it. Adding it to your home screen makes that far less likely, and a backup file makes it harmless.
